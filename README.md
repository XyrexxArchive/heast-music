## About

Heast Music is a lightweight Android music player focused on music discovery, smooth playback and a personal music library.

## Features

- YouTube Music search, discovery, suggestions and recommendations
- Background playback, queue management and radio mix
- Synced lyrics, plain lyrics and translation
- Personalized home shelves, moods and discovery
- Favorites, playlists and listening history
- Offline downloads and playback controls
- Home-screen widgets, media notifications and floating Dynamic Island player
- Three-stage floating Dynamic Island with compact, expanded and large modes, spring-like resizing and app launch
- Sleep timer and audio output selection
- Midnight liquid-glass theme with translucent surfaces, cool navy depth and icy-blue accents
- Lightweight image loading and caching

- Redesigned poster and lyrics share-card templates with gallery export to Pictures/Heast-Music.
- Collage Player with up to five tracks, adjustable background position and blur, card rotation, playback controls, progress and Heast watermark.
- In-app GitHub release update checker.

## Technology

Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, AndroidX Media3, OkHttp, NewPipeExtractor, Coil, Coroutines and Rust.
